Before you travel

Tell us whether you are coming for admissions, course guidance or a look around, and confirm a time by phone or email. For admissions advice, bring your subject results and a list of the programmes you are considering.

Write to the registrar

This form opens your email app with your message ready to send. To apply formally, use the application form.

Enter your name.

Enter an email address like name@example.com.

Enter a subject for your message.

Write your question or message.

Your email app should now be open. Check your message and press Send. If nothing opened, email info@kiitec.ac.tz directly.

What should I include?

Your name, a phone number, the programme you are interested in and your question. For entry guidance, mention your education level and main subjects.

Does the form send my message automatically?

No. It prepares the message in your email app. Review it and press Send. If no app opens, email info@kiitec.ac.tz directly.

Can I ask about accommodation and fees?

Yes. The registrar can confirm hostel places, current fees, payment arrangements and the documents you need before enrolling.
