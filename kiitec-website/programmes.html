How the diploma works

Three years, three levels.

KIITEC diplomas follow the NACTVET National Technical Award (NTA) ladder. Each year builds on the last, and the final year ends with the Ordinary Diploma.

NTA Level 4

Year one. Basic technician certificate: safety, core theory and your first lab work.

NTA Level 5

Year two. Technician certificate: deeper specialism, larger projects and industrial attachment.

NTA Level 6

Year three. Ordinary Diploma: advanced practice and a final project, ready for work or further study.

Before you choose

Which programme is right for my results?

Requirements depend on the programme. Share your school level, subjects, results and any earlier technical training with the registrar, who will advise you before you choose.

Where can I find fees and intake dates?

Contact the registrar for the current fee schedule and intakes. Ask which costs are included, and whether there are extra costs for materials, accommodation or registration.

Do students do industrial attachment?

Yes. Projects and industrial attachment are part of the diploma, so you practise your skills in a real workplace before you graduate.
