A working campus near Mount Meru.

Fifteen acres in Suye, Arusha, with labs, workshops, solar arrays, a student hostel and space to play. About 6 km from the city centre.

Facilities

Where you'll learn and live

  • Computer labs with rows of networked PCs for programming, design and data work.
  • Automation workshop with multi-PLC workbenches, motor drives and electro-pneumatic trainers.
  • Electronics and telecoms lab with 5G training kits, oscilloscopes and training modules.
  • Renewable energy lab with solar, wind and energy storage trainers, plus solar arrays on campus.
  • Innovation lab with a 3D printer and space for student projects.
  • Student hostel with room for up to 300 students.
  • Sports, including an outdoor basketball court.

Plan a visit

Come and see the labs.

KIITEC is in Suye (Moshono area), about 6 km from Arusha city centre. You can get here by dala-dala, bodaboda or taxi.

Call or email before you travel to confirm visiting hours and to arrange to meet the right person. If you are coming for admissions advice, bring your school results.
