OpenAnnounced September 2026

1,000 TV-ET scholarships from the Vodacom Tanzania Foundation

Sponsorship for NTA Level 4 training at Don Bosco KIITEC, Arusha. The application form is free. It helps the selection board assess each applicant's academic and financial need.

How to apply

  • Fill in the form completely, in CAPITAL LETTERS.
  • Attach copies of your birth certificate, CSEE (Form IV) result slip and VETA NVA III certificate.
  • Include your parents' or guardian's details and evidence of need.
  • Get the recommendations signed by your village chairperson and a religious leader.

Incomplete forms, or forms without the required documents, will be rejected. Bring your original documents if you are called for an interview.

Fully funded Level III scholarship from EACOP

EACOP, with Kent PLC and Inspecta Africa, funded a 12-month Level III programme for qualified KIITEC Level II graduates, starting October 2026. Applications for this round have closed. Watch this page for future rounds.

Programmes

  • Electrical & Industrial Automation Engineering
  • Electrical & Renewable Energy Engineering

What it covered

  • Tuition, stationery and uniform
  • Field or industrial training
  • Medical insurance and accommodation
  • Meals and transport

Who could apply

  • Tanzanian citizens aged 28 or younger
  • Holders of the Certificate of Secondary Education
  • KIITEC Level II graduates with a relevant Level II certificate

Women were especially encouraged to apply.

News

NewsAugust 2025

KIITEC joins the Salesian higher education network

Don Bosco KIITEC has joined the network of Salesian Institutions of Higher Education (IUS), which links Salesian universities and colleges around the world. It reflects a shared commitment to academic quality, innovation and the all-round formation of young people.

The institute's director, Fr. Sebastian Chirayath, has set out a master plan focused on emerging digital technologies, with diploma and degree courses for more than 3,000 students within five years. KIITEC staff and students also showed their work, including a bottling plant model, at the Arusha Industrial Expo.
