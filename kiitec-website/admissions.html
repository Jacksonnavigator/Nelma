From application to your first day

Submitting the form starts the conversation. The institute confirms admission and enrolment.

  1. Send the form

    Fill in the form below. It opens your email app with your details ready, so you can check them and press Send.

  2. Discuss your application

    The registrar contacts you about eligibility, documents, programme places and your preferred intake. Keep your phone on.

  3. Confirm joining

    Get your confirmed fees, payment instructions, reporting date and hostel arrangements, then prepare to join.

You don't upload anything here, but keep copies ready. The registrar will tell you which are needed for your programme.

  • Form IV (CSEE) result slip or certificate
  • Form VI (ACSEE) results or NVA / VETA certificates, if you have them
  • Birth certificate, NIDA number or passport
  • Passport-size photos
  • Parent or guardian contact details

Application questions

Can I apply if I'm not sure I'm eligible?

Yes. Send the form with your qualifications and interests, or contact the registrar. The institute confirms eligibility for each programme.

Does sending the form confirm my place?

No. The institute reviews your details and confirms admission, programme places and joining requirements.

Do I have to pay to apply?

No. Applying online is free, and no scholarship ever asks for payment. If anyone asks you to pay to secure a sponsored place, contact the institute directly.

How do I ask about sponsorship?

Choose the scholarship in the form or mention it in your message. Ask the registrar about current opportunities, eligibility and deadlines.
