Why KIITEC exists

It started with a broken lift.

In the early 2000s, a group of French engineers staying at a hotel in Arusha noticed that broken lifts and fax machines sat idle for weeks, waiting for technicians to fly in from abroad.

So in 2004, with the philanthropist Francis Brochon, they founded an institute to train those technicians here in Tanzania. Today the Salesians of Don Bosco run KIITEC, and its graduates install, maintain and design the systems that keep businesses running.

Frequently asked questions

What can I study at KIITEC?

Seven three-year NACTVET diplomas in engineering, a one-year professional course in IT and security systems, and short courses in electrical installation and computer applications. See Programmes.

How long are the courses?

Diplomas take three years and lead to an NTA Level 6 award. The professional course takes about one year. Short courses take about two to six months.

How much are the fees?

Fees are kept affordable and can be paid in instalments. Ask the registrar for the current fee schedule for your programme and intake.

Is there accommodation on campus?

Yes. The student hostel has room for up to 300 students. Ask the registrar about availability when you apply.

Are there scholarships?

Yes. Sponsors such as the Vodacom Tanzania Foundation and EACOP fund places at KIITEC. Current calls are listed on Scholarships & news. You never pay to apply.

Can I visit before I apply?

Yes. Call or email to arrange a visit. The campus is in Suye, about 6 km from Arusha city centre. See Contact for directions.

Still deciding?

Tell the registrar your school level, the subjects you enjoy and what kind of work you want to do. They will help you choose the right route.
